What We Do

Community Welfare

Supporting the Indian community through various initiatives.

Education Support

Scholarships, student support and educational guidance.

Social Services

Helping individuals and families in times of need.

Indian Culture & Heritage

Preserving and promoting our rich cultural heritage.

Sports & Recreation

Encouraging sports, fitness and healthy lifestyle.

Networking

Building strong connections within the community.

Key Facilities & Amenities

Badminton Arena

Multiple international standard indoor courts equipped with professional wooden flooring and lighting.

Olympic Pool

A temperature-controlled swimming pool with dedicated coaching programs and separate timings for families.

Library & Study Room

A quiet, cozy library housing over 10,000 books, research journals, daily newspapers, and high-speed Wi-Fi.

Multi-Purpose Auditorium

A spacious, fully air-conditioned auditorium with premium acoustics and seating capacity for major community events.
